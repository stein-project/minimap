# Minimap 0.1.0: the minimap in a mod of its own

The minimap leaves the HUD and becomes a separate mod, as an official Stein Project extra.

- **Everything kept**: the place on the screen, the size and the options of whoever used the minimap in the HUD come along.
- **Terrain or radar**: detailed terrain or radar only, square or round, with players, mobs and spawners.
- **Servers**: a server can turn off the minimap while you play there.
